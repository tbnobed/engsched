---
name: Ticket layout sizing
description: Keep the ticket workspace readable and sized for wide monitors.
---
Use the available content width for the ticket page, rather than a capped, centered layout. Keep conversation text readable and give the right-hand cards a proportional share of the width.

**Why:** The user rejected both tiny fixed-width cards and a centered page with large unused margins when reviewing the ticket redesign.

**How to apply:** Preserve this sizing approach during future ticket-page styling changes; stack the columns on narrow screens instead of shrinking the text.
